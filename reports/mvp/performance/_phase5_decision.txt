Phase 5 — num_ctx + num_predict tightening (4-case probe, demo-v2)

Observed per-stage token usage from Phase 4 (num_predict=2200 gen/rev, 900 audit):

  stage              prompt_tokens (max)   output_tokens (max)
  generation         3969                 1404
  audit              4385                  675
  revision           4209                 2105  (1 case at cap)
  revision_audit     4332                  675

Trial 1: num_ctx 8192 → 6144, audit num_predict 900 → 800
  strict mean   95.4s → 99.4s    +4% (worse)
  prefill delta < 0.3s per call  (eval is the bottleneck, not prefill)
  audit eval budget 800/900 hits at most 675 tokens observed → no headroom pressure

Conclusion: num_ctx=8192 and audit num_predict=900 are kept as the runtime
defaults. The cost of reducing them is zero-or-negative on this model and
hardware (RTX 4060 Laptop 8 GiB WDDM, qwen3.5:9b-q4_K_M at ~30-40 tok/s).
The decisive lever is prompt token count, not max_output. Phase 6 shrinks
the prompts by reducing the evidence fan-out (6 → 4 chunks, budget
11000 → 6000-8000 chars).

audit_num_predict and num_ctx remain CLI-tunable on evaluate_mvp.py so
future model swaps can re-measure without code changes.
