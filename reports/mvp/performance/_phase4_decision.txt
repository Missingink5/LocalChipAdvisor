Phase 4 — pipeline profile decision (4-case probe, demo-v2 manifest)

  strict   95.4s mean   4/4 ANSWERED   0 MODEL_ERROR   default (DEFAULT_PIPELINE_PROFILE="strict")
  audit    46.0s mean   3/4 ANSWERED   1 INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE  (lt8610.quiescent: hard issues rescued by strict revision, lost by audit drop)
  fast     37.8s mean   3/4 ANSWERED   1 INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE  (same lt8610.quiescent case)

Decision: keep strict as the MVP default. The 1/4 INSUFFICIENT case in
audit/fast is a real correctness regression (lt8610 quiescent current
question is recoverable by the model with a revision pass). Switching
to a faster profile trades acceptance correctness for ~2x speed; that
trade is rejected by the performance-fix contract ("MUST NOT gain
speed by removing evidence constraints, faking answers, or skipping
deterministic validation"). 2.2x from strict + the headroom in Phase 5
(num_predict tightening, prompts already 3377-4382 tokens against
num_ctx=8192) and Phase 6 (evidence 6→4 should remove ~30% of
prompt_tokens) gets us to the 3x target without changing quality.

Side finding fixed during Phase 4:
- revision occasionally emitted duplicate claim_id="claim_1" — qwen3.5
  regenerates the same id under JSON schema constraint uniqueItems was
  not enforced on object arrays (grammar silently no-op). Fixed three
  layers: grammar uniqueItems on outer array (defense in depth),
  REVISION_SYSTEM prompt explicitly forbids duplicates, parse_draft
  deterministically drops later duplicates with a logged warning
  (single failure mode converted from fail-closed MODEL_ERROR to a
  recoverable draft).
